ABOUT

Evidence-driven research and teaching in digital forensics.

Dr. Kiranbhai R. Dodiya is a Teaching and Research Associate in the Department of Biochemistry and Forensic Science at Gujarat University, Ahmedabad. His academic and research specialization is Digital Forensics, with professional interests spanning digital forensic investigation, malware analysis, cybercrime investigation, cybersecurity, incident response, digital evidence, and emerging technologies in forensic science.

His work bridges the classroom, the research laboratory, and operational practice. He has taught and trained across academic institutions and government-oriented environments, delivered invited lectures on cybercrime, cybersecurity, digital forensics, incident response, firewall/IDS concepts, and cybercrime investigation, and contributed to police and cybercrime awareness programs, forensic workshops, and academic conferences.

His research addresses ATM malware, malware analysis, digital evidence, image forensics, phishing, AI and cybersecurity, and the emerging challenges facing digital forensic practice.
